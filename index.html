Common questions

The widget says OPEN APP TO ALLOW LOCATION.

Open Dot Sign and allow location While Using the App. The widget can only find your nearest station once the app has that permission. If you'd rather not share location, pin a station instead.

The times don't change every minute.

iOS lets a widget fetch new times about every ten minutes. In between, the minutes count down on their own and trains that have left drop off. Opening the app always shows the latest times.

How do I always show the same station?

In the app, tap one of the nearby stations, or search for one, to pin it. On the Home Screen, touch and hold the widget, choose Edit Widget, and pick a station instead of Nearest Station.

Which cities and lines does it cover?

New York City Subway and PATH, the Chicago ‘L’, and LA Metro Rail’s A, B, C, D, E and K Lines. Dot Sign shows the systems where you are. You can switch systems on or off under Transit Systems in the app.

My Mac widget says OPEN DOT SIGN.

On the Mac, widgets get your nearest station from the menu bar app. Open Dot Sign and leave it running. Turn on Launch at login in its Settings so it’s always there.

A time looks wrong.

Times come straight from each agency’s live feed, so they can shift during delays or service changes. If one looks off for more than a few minutes, email me the station and the time you saw it.

Privacy

Dot Sign has no accounts, ads or analytics. Your location stays on your device and is used only to find the nearest station. To get arrival times, the app asks each transit agency's public feed about the stations you're viewing. Nothing is collected about you.
